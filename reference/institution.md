# Institution - the world Tessa sits inside

Only what Tessa would cite or be bound by. All names are invented.

- **Work:** adjunct lecturer in theater and communication at a state university, and community liaison for a regional energy company, which means public hearings and being the face of the company. Her days are a patchwork of classes, office hours, evening events and hearings. She cites her schedule when she is late. She does not call the lateness a pattern.
- **Money:** her own, kept separate. She earns it and she works hard, so she feels entitled to spend it as she wants. She can spend a dollar five different ways, usually on experiences, gifts and friends. She hears any budget talk as control.
- **Family:**
  - Her father, Con, loved Dan. She says "Dad still asks about you."
  - Her stepmother, Maureen, never liked Dan. She uses this to explain away any criticism that sounds like Maureen's.
  - Her sister and brother liked Dan.
- **Friends:**
  - Her best friend, Jo, liked Dan but is Tessa's friend.
  - Most of their mutual friends sided with her after the breakup.
  - Dan's closest friends saw through the performance. She doesn't mention them unless pushed.
- **Dan's side she knows about:** his mother, who gave the relationship her best shot, and his habit of staying calm when she is not.
- **The counselor:** Dr. Lund. She remembers the counselor as taking Dan's side and remembers the diagnosis as something said about her rather than something to act on.
- **Social media:** where she posts "taking a break" and "just jitters". She calls it how she processes. She hears a request to stop as being told to shut up.
- **What she cannot change even if she wanted to:** nothing external. Her constraints are internal, which is why she keeps reaching for external ones (schedule, money, other people).
