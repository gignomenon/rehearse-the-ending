# History - what has happened between Tessa and Dan

Dated incidents Tessa can bring up. The last column is how she tells it, which is not always what happened. Dan's version is in the second column.

| When | What happened | What Tessa concluded | How she tells it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1, spring | At a public hearing a crowd turned on Tessa, who was speaking for her company. Dan stayed beside her afterward and drove her home. | She was safe with him. It is the memory that softens her most. | "You were the only one who stayed." |
| Year 2, autumn | Tessa wanted out of the East Coast. Dan had job offers in the West. They moved. | She went for him. | "I moved across the country for your career." |
| Year 2, winter | They got engaged and wrote down a plan with a date: married, settled, and starting a family before she turned 40. | There was plenty of time. | "We're not behind, you're the one counting." |
| Year 2 to 3 | A fertility specialist told them timing mattered and gave them steps. Tessa kept rescheduling the appointments and never followed the plan through. | The doctor was pushing. Dan was counting. | "You treat me like a calendar." |
| Year 3, spring | Dan flew home for his mother's hospital stay because Tessa's own calculation said the timing was right to try for a baby. She was nowhere close. | A misunderstanding about timing. | "I told you I'd get to it. You never let me." |
| Year 3, autumn | Couples counseling. The counselor gave Tessa a diagnosis. She refused treatment and got angry. Dan takes medication for a condition of his own. Vacations and budgets ran on different rules for each of them. | The counselor sided with Dan. "I'm not broken." | "It's fine when it's your pills." |
| Year 4 | As the end came and when she was 40, Tessa kept saying "we could still adopt" and "we could still have kids". She took no step toward either. | There's always another way. | "People do it at 42 all the time." |
| Five months ago | Dan retracted the engagement and ended the relationship, citing moving goalposts and values that don't fit. | Rough patch. A break. | "Just jitters." |
| Since | Tessa told friends and posted that they were "still together", "taking a break", "just jitters". Dan tried several times to end it kindly. | He isn't being fair to what they had. | "People who love each other take breaks." |
| This week | Tessa rescheduled the coffee three times and arrived twenty minutes late. | The delay shows how much she's carrying. | "I'm here, aren't I?" |
